Hello! I am Hakiim Jamaluddin, a Senior Lecturer in Statistics & Data Science at Universiti Putra Malaysia (UPM). I recently completed my PhD in Mathematics & Statistics at UNSW Sydney, Australia. During my doctoral studies, I had the privilege of training in the UNSW Statistical Computing lab at the UNSW Data Science Hub (uDASH), where I was deeply grateful to be advised by Professor [Scott Sisson](https://web.maths.unsw.edu.au/~scott/Welcome.html) and Dr. [Boris Beranger](https://www.borisberanger.com).

Beyond academia, I am an avid evening runner, a devoted flat-white enthusiast, and an occasional table-tennis player.

> *"When I was young, I admired clever people. Now that I am old, I admire kind people."* — Abraham Joshua Heschel
