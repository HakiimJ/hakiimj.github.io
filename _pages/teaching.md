It has been a privilege to teach, mentor, and collaborate with students across multiple institutions in Australia, Malaysia, and Indonesia. My journey in statistics, data science, and related fields has been deeply rewarding. Below is an overview of the academic roles I have held and the courses I have had the opportunity to teach.

### Universiti Putra Malaysia (UPM), Selangor, Malaysia
- **Senior Lecturer in Statistics and Data Science**, Department of Mathematics & Statistics (October 2025 – Present)
  
  Currently, I have the honor of contributing to undergraduate and postgraduate programs through lecturing and course development, striving to equip students with robust analytical skills and a deep appreciation for data.
  
  *Courses Taught:*
  - *STS3401 - Probability & Statistics 1*: 2025/26 (Sem-1), 2026/27 (Sem-1)
  - *STS3404 - Data Science with Applications*: 2025/26 (Sem-2) — [View student projects](https://hakiim-j.shinyapps.io/Data-Science-Projects/)
  - *STS3409 - Computational Statistics*: 2025/26 (Sem-1), 2026/27 (Sem-1)

### Universitas Airlangga (UNAIR), Indonesia
- **Guest Lecturer on Data Science and Machine Learning**, Department of Mathematics (2025 – Present)
  
  I am grateful for the opportunity to share insights on data science and machine learning with students as a guest lecturer.

### University of New South Wales (UNSW), Sydney, Australia
- **Project Advisor (Work Integrated Learning, WIL)**, Collaboration with SAS Institute Australia, School of Mathematics and Statistics (August 2024 – November 2024)
  
  Guided students through industry-integrated projects, helping them apply data analysis and SAS tools to real-world challenges in collaboration with the SAS Institute.
- **Casual Academic (Health Data Science)**, School of Biomedical Sciences (March 2023 – 2024)
  
  Supported the teaching of health data analysis, fostering students' understanding of statistical methods and machine learning applications in biomedicine.

### University of Technology Sydney (UTS), Sydney, Australia
- **Casual Academic (Marketing Research Methods)**, Business School (July 2024 – October 2024)
  
  Facilitated tutorials on marketing research methodologies, helping business students bridge the gap between statistical analysis and practical applications.

### Canterbury Institute of Management (CIM), Sydney, Australia
- **Casual Lecturer (Business Statistics)**, School of Business (February 2024 – June 2024)
  
  Enjoyed the opportunity to deliver lectures on business statistics, guiding students through probability, inference, and practical data interpretation.

### Macquarie University (MQ), Sydney, Australia
- **Casual Academic (Probability and Statistics)**, School of Mathematical and Physical Sciences (February 2022 – May 2022)
  
  Had the pleasure of introducing undergraduate students to the foundational concepts of probability and statistics.
